# Pixel Pals outcomes

- [ ] **Seven customizable companions and browser play area.** Provide seven configurable original pals (Mochi, Koko, Jiji, Tae, Nini, Rumi, Sunny), each with editable id, name, signature color, accessory, personality and job (water, study, stretch, eye-rest, snack, posture or cheer). Generate character sprites in code on a 32×32 grid; do not require external character images. Include idle, walk (left/right flip), sleep with floating z's, happy/jump/hearts, sad/droopy, group dance, eating/drinking and being-dragged poses. Pals walk across the lower area, pause/sit/sleep, sometimes follow cursor-like movement, can be dragged/dropped with a small bounce, line up for an hourly 10-second group dance, nap more often after 30 minutes without interaction, and wake when clicked. Clicking a pal opens their games.

- [ ] **Reminders and focus controls.** Deliver 100+ varied reminder messages grouped by buddy job. Default interval is 15 minutes and is configurable. A random eligible pal shows a reminder with “Done ✓” (rewards hearts and happy reaction) and “Snooze 5 min”; play a generated soft pixel chime. Support quiet hours and a Do Not Disturb/focus toggle. In the approved browser app, show notifications and controls in-app rather than OS notification/tray integration.

- [ ] **Six playable mini-games.** A click on a character opens an arcade menu to select or randomly launch: (1) a quiz backed by 40 sample questions across Python basics, maths, physics, environmental studies and general knowledge, with categories and a simple in-app Add Question form; (2) Memory Match with character faces; (3) a 3×3 sliding puzzle with a pixel picture; (4) Math Sprint with five questions in 30 seconds; (5) Rhythm Tap with D/F/J/K; and (6) Spot the Difference or a Wordle-style word game. Games have difficulty levels, score and timer where relevant, plus clicked-pal win/loss reactions.

- [ ] **Progression and routine features.** Completed reminders and won games award hearts/stars. Track a daily streak and let pals appear sad when ignored all day. Add achievements including first water break, a 7-day streak and 10 quizzes won, each with a popup. Add a wardrobe/shop spending hearts on hats, outfits and backgrounds, with purchases visibly applied as pixel overlays. Include a Pomodoro focus/break timer set to 25/5 minutes.

- [ ] **Browser settings and persistent state.** Persist progress between sessions. Include reminder interval, quiet hours, active-character selection, 1×/2×/3× character size, sound volume and browser-compatible notification/focus controls. Keep Electron-only features (transparent always-on-top overlay, system tray, native notifications, start-on-boot and desktop monitor selection) out of the browser adaptation; supply visible in-app navigation/actions instead.

- [ ] **Browser-ready launch and delivery.** Replace the inherited maze game and loading artwork with Pixel Pals, keep a game-specific favicon and sharing title/description, make the app playable from its browser preview, and save a verified managed checkpoint after the applicable build and pack checks.
